Feature deep dive · Steps 01–02

Vyaya asks, calculates, and lets you edit

Setup is a short set of questions. From the answers Vyaya works out your calories, protein and every other target itself. Nothing is fixed: each number can be changed later.

What Vyaya asks

  • Age, sex, height, current weight
  • Target weight and pace: 0.25, 0.5 or 0.75 kg a week
  • Your day outside workouts: mostly sitting, on your feet, or physical
  • How many sessions a week, and which types
  • Diet type (vegetarian with egg) and foods to avoid
  • Meals per day, wake-up and bedtime, for reminders

How it calculates

  • Resting burn: Mifflin-St Jeor formula from weight, height, age and sex
  • Maintenance: resting burn × an activity factor from your answers
  • Calorie target: maintenance minus the deficit for your pace (0.5 kg a week ≈ 550 kcal a day), never below your resting burn
  • Protein: 1.6–2.0 g per kg. Fat: about 25% of calories. Carbs: the rest.
  • Fibre: 14 g per 1,000 kcal. Water: 35 ml per kg.

Example: 75 kg, 175 cm, 30 years gives a resting burn of about 1,700, maintenance about 2,450, target about 1,900 kcal with 135 g protein.

Editing later

  • Profile → Targets: change any number, or re-answer the questions
  • A number you set by hand is marked Custom and stays put
  • Reset to calculated brings Vyaya's figure back
  • Every 4 weeks, or after a 2 kg change, Vyaya proposes updated targets. You accept or keep the old ones.
  • Reaching your target weight offers the switch to maintenance

Chart instructions become app behaviour

  • Bejad roti (wheat 3 kg, barley 1 kg, black chana ½ kg) saved as its own recipe
  • Oil 2 tsp a day: an oil counter on the day view
  • Water 3–4 litres: the daily water target
  • Last meal 2 hours before sleep: a cut-off reminder
  • Fixed meal times: reminders at the chart's times

Suggest me

One tap on any empty meal. Vyaya picks from your recipes what fits the calories and protein left today, follows the eat-and-avoid rules, and skips what you ate yesterday.

Dinner idea: kala chana, 1 katori dal, 2 bejad roti ≈ 560 kcal · 27 g protein

Calories: Vyaya calculates

The printed chart is set at 1,200 kcal, 55 g protein, and by my rough estimate your whiteboard days come to about 1,200–1,700 kcal. Vyaya keeps the chart's foods and rules but sets the amounts from your own answers, and scales portions to match.

Scan your nutritionist's chart

Build your own

Add meals, set times, add foods from search, your recipes or quick entries. Totals update live against your targets while you build.

Chart variations

    Feature deep dive · Steps 08–09

    Share like Strava: stories, transparent stickers, route maps

    Every finished session can leave the app three ways. Runs, walks, rides and hikes carry their GPS path with them.

    Transparent sticker

    Distance5.2 km
    Pace5:48 /km
    Time30:10

    Stats and route with no background, saved as a PNG or copied. Paste it over any photo or video in Instagram, the way Strava stickers work. Sample figures.

    Instagram Stories

    Your photo plus an overlay goes straight into the Stories composer at 9:16. Overlay text switches between light and dark to stay readable on the photo.

    Gym, yoga and home sessions

    No route, so the sticker shows duration, calories, kg lifted, PR badges and the muscles worked.

    GPS paths

    Recorded on the phone for runs, walks, rides and hikes. Drawn as a line, optionally coloured by pace, with km markers and splits.

    Map styles

    Route onlyStandard mapSatelliteDark map

    Maps come from Apple Maps on the phone. No account or key needed.

    Hide your home

    Optionally trim the first and last 200 m of a route before sharing, so the start point isn't shown.

    How calories burned are estimated
