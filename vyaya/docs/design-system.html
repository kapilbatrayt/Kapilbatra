Principles

From your moodboard
01

One signal colour

Black or paper, plus a single red. Orange appears only as the tail of a red gradient. Nothing else gets colour.

02

Blur means movement

Photos smear in one direction with part of the subject held sharp. Interface elements stay crisp; only images and transitions blur.

03

The lockup

Small mono label, mark, small mono label, on one line across the middle. It signs every share card.

04

Poster type

Heavy uppercase grotesk set tight for headlines and numbers. Tiny wide-tracked mono for everything that labels.

05

Hairlines, not boxes

Sections are divided by 1 px rules. Corners are square or 2 px. Shadows are not used.

06

Grain

A light film grain sits on photos and gradients so they feel printed. Never on text or controls.

Shape

Radius 0–2 px. Chips are the one fully round element. 1 px hairlines; 1.5 px on controls.

Spacing

4-point scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 40, 72. Screen margin 20. Touch targets 48 minimum.

Motion

Screens change with a 160 ms sideways smear. A new PR drags a red streak across the row. Off when Reduce Motion is on.

Blur area

Smear direction
How the app will do it
  1. Cut the person out of the photo with Apple's Vision framework, on the phone.
  2. Smear only the person in one direction (Core Image motion blur through the person's exact outline). The background stays sharp.
  3. Map brightness to a two-colour gradient.
  4. Add grain, then the lockup and stats.

All on the phone. Four grades at launch: Signal, Dusk, Paper and Chrome.
