# Script: Crazy things people built with Claude

**Title (hook card):** People are building WILD stuff with Claude?!
Alt 1: Someone made a MUSIC VIDEO. In pure code!
Alt 2: Claude is busy? Play DOOM!

**Mode:** B (Varun-style tech breakdown) · ~190 words · ~1:15

---

## Read-aloud script

People are building absolutely insane things with Claude right now. And I don't mean apps. I mean stuff that shouldn't be possible from a prompt.

First one. Claude Code is slow sometimes, right? You sit there waiting. So a developer built a mod that drops you into a live multiplayer Doom server while Claude is busy. And here's the twist. Every other player in that match is also a person waiting for their Claude to finish. Yeah. A lobby of people waiting on AI.

Second. A full music video about AI doom. Every single frame is painted in code. No editor. No footage. Just p5.js, a headless Chrome, and ffmpeg stitching it together.

Third. A fishing game that runs in your browser on raw WebGPU. Not a game engine. Raw shaders. Written with Opus 5.5.

But that's not the crazy part. Someone collected 475 viral videos made with Opus 5.5, with the prompts. And people are already building Claude plugins that scan Reddit, YouTube and TikTok to tell you what content to post next.

What's impressive here is nobody's waiting for permission anymore. One prompt, one weekend, one finished thing.

Clean.
